# Homepage: "What I'm working on".
---
Most of my time goes to [Geneial](https://geneial.com), which I founded in 2020. We build research infrastructure for rare and genetic diseases: registries, natural-history studies, and the data systems behind them, for patient organizations, researchers, and therapy developers.

The problems I spend the most time on:

- **Keeping participants connected.** We can sequence someone’s entire genome and then struggle to ask them a follow-up question. People should be able to join research once, stay involved for years, decide how their data is used, and see what comes of it.
- **Data that holds up as evidence.** Precision medicine needs precise trials, and those need standardized but detailed clinical data. Registries should produce longitudinal data that researchers and drug developers can analyze without rebuilding it for every study.
- **Privacy built in, not bolted on.** Rare-disease data is sparse and easy to re-identify, so encryption and consent have to be part of the design.
- **AI that does real research work.** Agents that turn messy surveys and datasets into standard, analyzable formats, tested in peer review rather than just in demos.

For what Geneial offers, [geneial.com](https://geneial.com) has the details.
