# Homepage: "What I'm working on".
---
Since 2020, I’ve led [Geneial](https://geneial.com), where we’re building what I think precision medicine has been missing: a lasting relationship between patients and the researchers who study their conditions.

- **Registries and natural-history studies** that people can stay part of for years, with consent, surveys, and follow-up in one place.
- **Longitudinal, research-ready data** that researchers and therapy developers can analyze without rebuilding it for every study.
- **AI agents** that turn how families describe symptoms, and the messy formats data arrives in, into standard terms research can use.

Registries for patient communities including Xia-Gibbs syndrome and the HNRNP-related disorders run on Geneial today.
