# Homepage: "Outside work".
---
Music has been a constant since high school, when I played in New Hampshire’s All-State honors jazz band. You can hear my band, Pändo, on [*Trembling Giant*](https://music.apple.com/us/album/trembling-giant-ep/739535020) (Apple Music) and the [*Vagabond* EP](https://open.spotify.com/album/6HLv7X1LKQWxAdIZvO13tf) (Spotify). I play drums, piano, guitar, bass, and a Suzuki Omnichord, and I’ll pick up just about any percussion or stringed instrument within reach, ukulele included.

For the past four years I’ve volunteered teaching an English as a second language course for native Chinese speakers at my local church congregation. I speak Mandarin and Spanish, and I like basketball, travel, cooking, and learning about other cultures.
